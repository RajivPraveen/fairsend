# Pre-survey (baseline)

Column names for the analysis are in `[brackets]`.

**Part A: How you move money**

1. Participant code `[code]`
2. In the last 12 months, did you send money abroad, receive money from abroad, or both? (Send / Receive / Both / Neither) `[direction]`
3. Which country is the money usually sent **from**? `[from_country]` And **to**? `[to_country]`
4. How often? (Weekly / Every two weeks / Monthly / Every two months / Quarterly / Twice a year / Once a year) `[frequency]`
5. Typical amount per transfer, in USD or your currency (number + currency) `[typical_amount]`, `[typical_currency]`
6. Which service do you or your family usually use? (open text) `[current_provider]`
7. Have you paid tuition or rent from abroad in one large transfer? (Yes / No) `[large_transfer]`
   If yes: roughly how much, and how? (bank wire / money transfer app / tuition payment platform / other) `[large_method]`

**Part B: What you know about costs**

8. When you send money, what do you pay? (choose all that apply: a fee / a worse exchange rate than the "real" rate /
   taxes / nothing / not sure) `[costs_known]`
9. Have you heard of the "mid-market" exchange rate? (Yes, and I could explain it / Heard of it / No) `[knows_midmarket]`
10. True or false: "A transfer with no fee costs nothing." (True / False / Not sure) `[zero_fee_quiz]`
11. About how much did your **last** transfer cost in total, as a % of the amount? (0–1% / 1–3% / 3–5% / 5–10% / >10% / no idea) `[perceived_cost]`
12. How confident are you that you use a good-value service? (1 = not at all … 5 = very) `[confidence_pre]`
13. How do you usually choose a service? (choose all: lowest fee / best rate / speed / family uses it / convenience / bank I already have / other) `[choice_factors]`
