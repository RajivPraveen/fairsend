# Participant information and consent

**Study:** How students send and receive money across borders, and whether a cost comparison tool helps.

**What you'll do:** a 5-minute survey, a 20-minute session trying FairSend (comparing providers, checking a past
transfer receipt if you have one, trying tuition mode, and optionally setting an exchange-rate alert), a 5-minute
follow-up survey, and one short survey 4 weeks later.

**Your data:**
- Survey answers are linked only to a random participant code, not your name or email.
- If you check a receipt, it is processed on the researcher's laptop and never saved. You may crop out names and
  account numbers first, or type the numbers in instead.
- If you set a rate alert, your email is stored only to send the alert. You can delete the alert at any time, which
  removes your email.
- Results are reported only in aggregate.

**Voluntary:** you can skip any question or stop at any time without giving a reason.

**Not advice:** FairSend shows estimates from public data. It doesn't give financial advice, and nothing in the
session asks you to change providers.
