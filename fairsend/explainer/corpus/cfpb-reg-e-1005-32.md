## About this section
Section 1005.32 of Regulation E lists the limited situations in which a remittance transfer provider may give estimated figures, instead of exact figures, for items such as the exchange rate, other fees, and the total to recipient. Outside these exceptions, disclosures must be exact and accurate when payment is made (§ 1005.31(f)), and any estimate must be labeled "Estimated" (§ 1005.31(d)). The text below is quoted from the eCFR (Title 12, current as of the September 14, 2026 issue), with some sub-paragraphs omitted and long paragraphs condensed (cross-references removed).

## The temporary exception for banks and credit unions has expired
(a) Temporary exception for insured institutions—(1) General. Estimates may be provided for the amounts required to be disclosed under § 1005.31(b)(1)(iv) through (vii), if: (i) A remittance transfer provider cannot determine the exact amounts for reasons beyond its control; (ii) A remittance transfer provider is an insured institution; and (iii) The remittance transfer is sent from the sender's account with the institution.

(2) Sunset date. Paragraph (a)(1) of this section expires on July 21, 2020.

"Insured institution" means insured depository institutions (such as banks) and insured credit unions.

## Permanent exception: transfers to certain countries
(b) Permanent exceptions—(1) Permanent exception for transfers to certain countries—(i) General. Estimates may be provided for transfers to certain countries for the amounts required to be disclosed under § 1005.31(b)(1)(iv) through (b)(1)(vii), if a remittance transfer provider cannot determine the exact amounts when the disclosure is required because:

(A) The laws of the recipient country do not permit such a determination, or

(B) The method by which transactions are made in the recipient country does not permit such determination.

(ii) Safe harbor. A remittance transfer provider may rely on the list of countries published by the Bureau to determine whether estimates may be provided under paragraph (b)(1) of this section, unless the provider has information that a country's laws or the method by which transactions are conducted in that country permits a determination of the exact disclosure amount.

## Permanent exception: transfers scheduled five or more business days ahead
(2) Permanent exception for transfers scheduled before the date of transfer. (i) Estimates may be provided for the amounts to be disclosed under §§ 1005.31(b)(1)(iv) through (vii) if the remittance transfer is scheduled by a sender five or more business days before the date of the transfer. In addition, if, at the time the sender schedules such a transfer, the provider agrees to a sender's request to fix the amount to be transferred in the currency in which the remittance transfer will be received and not the currency in which it is funded, estimates may also be provided for the amounts to be disclosed under §§ 1005.31(b)(1)(i) through (iii).

## Permanent exception: recipient-side fees and foreign taxes
(3) Permanent exception for optional disclosure of non-covered third-party fees and taxes collected by a person other than the provider. Estimates may be provided for applicable non-covered third-party fees and taxes collected on the remittance transfer by a person other than the provider, which are permitted to be disclosed under § 1005.31(b)(1)(viii), provided such estimates are based on reasonable sources of information.

## Permanent exception: exchange rate estimates by small-volume banks and credit unions
(4) Permanent exception for estimation of the exchange rate by an insured institution. (i) Estimates may be provided for a remittance transfer to a particular country for the amounts required to be disclosed under § 1005.31(b)(1)(iv) through (vii), if the designated recipient of the remittance transfer will receive funds in the country's local currency and all of the following conditions are met:

(A) The remittance transfer provider is an insured institution;

(B) At the time the insured institution must provide the disclosure, the insured institution cannot determine the exact exchange rate required to be disclosed under § 1005.31(b)(1)(iv) for that remittance transfer;

(C) The insured institution made 1,000 or fewer remittance transfers in the prior calendar year to the particular country for which the designated recipients of those transfers received funds in the country's local currency; and

(D) The remittance transfer is sent from the sender's account with the insured institution.

## Permanent exception: covered third-party fees estimated by banks and credit unions
(5) Permanent exception for estimation of covered third-party fees by an insured institution. Under conditions set out in the regulation, an insured institution may provide estimates of covered third-party fees and the resulting total to recipient (§ 1005.31(b)(1)(vi) through (vii)) for a transfer to a particular designated recipient's institution when it cannot determine the exact covered third-party fees at the time the disclosure is required.

## What estimates mean for the sender
Because estimates are only allowed in these specific cases, a sender who sees the word "Estimated" next to the exchange rate, other fees, or total to recipient is looking at a figure the provider could not determine exactly at the time of the disclosure. In all other cases, the exchange rate and amounts shown before payment must be the ones actually used.
